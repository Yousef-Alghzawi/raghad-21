one

Before everything else, you.

Vincent painted almond blossoms for a child who had just arrived, because they are the first flowers of the year. They open before the leaves, before the season has made up its mind about anything.

That is what you are to my days, Raghad. The first thing. Before I am awake enough to want coffee, I want to know that you slept well. Before the day has decided what it will be, it has already decided to have you in it.

I did not notice it happening. One morning you were simply the axis, and everything else, the lectures, the rounds, the traffic on University Street, was just turning around you. You became the most essential part of my day without ever asking to be. That is the only way it could have happened.

two

This city has your name on every corner.

I tried, once, to drive through Irbid without thinking of you. I got as far as the first traffic light. Almost every corner of this city has become a place where you happened to me. Tap a blossom.

  • OZ, where the coffee was never the point, and I still remember what it tasted like.

  • Daiken. I could not tell you what I ate. I could tell you exactly what you were wearing.

  • Calma, which is also the word for what your voice does to a bad day.

  • Vogo, where the traffic outside was the only thing in a hurry. Inside, time did us the favour of slowing down.

  • The airplane. A plane that will never take off, and I still felt like I was going somewhere, because I was sitting next to you.

  • District 7, where the evening always went by faster than an evening should.

  • Cracko, and every craving I will never again be able to satisfy alone.

  • McDonald's. Even the fries taste like a memory now.

  • The east of the city and the south of it. Whole neighbourhoods I now measure by how far they are from you.

  • University Street, on the days we go together to pick up lunch for the family, and a ten-minute errand becomes the best part of my afternoon.

  • King Abdullah University Hospital, where the corridors are long and the shifts are longer, and where I learned that a white coat looks different when you are standing next to me in one.

  • JUST. I walked in to become a doctor and walked out having found you. I am still not sure which one I will be prouder of.

  • Culture Circle, which I cannot go around without slowing down, as if you might be standing there.

  • The cinema. Spider-Man, then The Odyssey. I watched you watch them. You were the better film both times.

three

The days that go wrong

There are days that go wrong, and I can tell you exactly why. I did not get to breathe you in. No trace of your scent on my sleeve, none left in the air of a room you had just walked out of.

Those days feel like a page with the first line missing. Everything after it is technically there, and none of it makes sense.

four

Every outfit, somehow, again

I do not know how you do it. Every time I think I have seen the most beautiful version of you, you walk in wearing another one. The burgundy that has become yours. The blue you love, and the blue that loves you back. A coat, a scarf, a plain black top on a Tuesday.

Each outfit is more gorgeous than the one before it, and the one before it was already unfair.

five

When you smile, the day is at its best.

My days are at their best when your smile and your laugh are shining. When you tell me a story, and the story is not even the point. The point is the way you tell it, and the way you look at me to check that I am still listening. I always am.

Your talks and your stories never stop ringing in my ears. I hear them in the car, on the ward, in the quiet before I sleep.

And every single day I see you, you are prettier than the last time. I keep thinking that has to stop being true at some point. It does not.

six

Rounds

Hospital rotations were supposed to be the hard part. Long corridors, early mornings, standing for hours pretending to understand what the attending meant. And they are the hard part.

But they will never be a bad memory, and that is because of you. You, at the end of the corridor. You, in a white coat, making the longest morning at King Abdullah University Hospital feel like a place I chose to be, instead of a place I had to be.

Without you there, none of it would be worth remembering.

seven

The soul that completes me

I used to think I was whole, and simply waiting for something good to happen to me. I was wrong about the first part.

You are the soul that completes me, Raghad. I only understood the shape of what was missing when you filled it. Now, when you are not here, it is not a small absence. It is a big piece of me, gone, out there somewhere in the city, wherever you are.

eight

What my heart refuses to put down

Every day spent with you became a memory my heart will not let go of. Not only the big ones. The ordinary ones, which turned out to be the big ones.

  • a lunch

    A lunch that ran long because neither of us wanted to be the one who checked the time.

  • a coffee date

    Your cup, my cup, and two hours that felt like twenty minutes.

  • a study session

    The notes were open, and I was reading you instead.

  • a pastry and a cup of tea

    A breakfast pastry and tea in the morning, which sounds like nothing and was everything.

  • an evening talk

    A talk that started about nothing and ended with me knowing you a little better and loving you a lot more.

nine

For the road

Two songs, because words fail me and Majida does not. The second one has the most accurate title ever written. مطرحك بقلبي. Your place is in my heart. It always was.

21 twenty-one

I saw you wearing them before I bought them. A gold bracelet on your wrist, a ring on your finger, and you, in my mind, looking the way you always do, which is to say gorgeous. Then I went out and found them.

They are gold-plated silver, on purpose. The silver one I gave you before deserves company, not competition. Now you have gold for the days that ask for gold, and silver for the days that ask for silver, and every outfit gets to choose.
